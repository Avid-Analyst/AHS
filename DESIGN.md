Rule1(Cycle Assembly): An arrival at a shovel followed immediately by a departure from the same shovel constitute a loading event

Rule2(Queue Calculation): nominal shovel loading takes 180 seonds(3 minutes). Any time spent beyond 180 seconds between arrival and departure is logged as Queue Delay.
Rule3(Payload Rule): Nominal truck capacity is 230 tonnes. Any payload excedding 253 tonnes(110%) gets flagged as is_overloaded=1.
